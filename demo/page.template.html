Custody

Prove what your AI did to a loan.

Every AI decision on a loan file is recorded, gated, and chained — so when an examiner, an investor, or a GSE counterparty asks what happened, the answer is an artifact they can verify themselves rather than an assurance they have to accept.

Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-04 took effect on 8 August 2026. Seller/servicers using AI or ML in origination, underwriting, servicing or QC must keep a per-decision record of principal, model, endpoint, redacted prompt, response treatment, policy version, decision outcome and timestamp — append-only and signed, queryable by loan, date, model and principal. Enforcement is repurchase risk.

What an examiner receives

The packet for one loan: every decision, in order, including the ones that were rejected. It carries the public key and the records, so the recipient verifies it without access to any of the lender’s systems.

What this is, and what it is not

It is a Python library and an append-only, hash-chained, Ed25519-signed ledger that captures every field LL-2026-04 names, plus a deterministic gate that decides the response_treatment on each output: figures must appear in a source document, fields must cite one, classifications must land in a closed set, and anything under the confidence floor goes to a person.

It is not legal advice and does not certify compliance. It does no bias or fair-lending testing — that is a separate obligation and a separate product. It writes to no loan origination system. And there is no model anywhere in the verification path: a model judging another model is not evidence.
